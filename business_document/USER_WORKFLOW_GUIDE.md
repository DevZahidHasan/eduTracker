# eduTracker: User Workflow Guide

Welcome to the **eduTracker Enterprise ERP**. This manual provides step-by-step instructions on how different staff members should use the system for their daily operations.

---

## 1. The Principal / Administrator Workflow

As the Administrator, you have full access to all modules and oversee the entire institution's health.

### 1.1. Reviewing the Daily Dashboard
1. Log in to the system.
2. The **Dashboard** is your command center. Every morning, review:
   * **Total Enrollment:** Ensure student numbers align with physical records.
   * **Daily Attendance:** Monitor the attendance rate. If it drops below your goal (e.g., 95%), investigate which classes have low attendance.
   * **Outstanding Dues:** Quickly check the financial health to see pending fee payments.

### 1.2. Updating School Settings
1. Navigate to **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Update the **Academic Year**, **School Name**, and **Contact Info**.
3. Upload the **School Logo**. This logo will automatically appear on all report cards, ID cards, and fee receipts.

### 1.3. Managing Licenses (If Locked Out)
1. If the screen shows "System Locked", your enterprise license has expired.
2. Contact your IT provider for a new License Key.
3. Paste the provided key into the lock screen and click **Activate License** to restore full access.

## 2. The Teacher Workflow

Teachers use eduTracker to manage their specific classes, record daily attendance, and enter marks.

### 2.1. Taking Daily Attendance
1. Navigate to the **Attendance** module.
2. Select your assigned **Class** and **Section**.
3. The date defaults to today. A list of your students will appear.
4. Mark each student as **Present, Absent, Late, or Excused**.
5. Click **Save Attendance**. *(Note: This data instantly updates the Principal's dashboard).*

### 2.2. Entering Exam Marks
1. Navigate to the **Marks** module.
2. Select the **Class**, **Section**, **Subject**, and **Exam Type** (e.g., Mid-Term).
3. Enter the marks for each student in the list.
4. Review your entries carefully. Once you click **Lock Marks**, you will not be able to edit them without Administrator approval.

### 2.3. Generating Question Papers
1. Navigate to **Question Papers**.
2. Click **Create New Paper**.
3. Select the Class, Subject, and Exam duration.
4. You can manually type questions, pull them from the **Question Bank**, or use an AI-assisted template.
5. Save and print the generated PDF.

## 3. The HR & Payroll Workflow

The Human Resources team manages staff records, leaves, and salary processing.

### 3.1. Onboarding New Staff
1. Navigate to the **HR** module -> **Staff Directory**.
2. Click **Add New Staff**.
3. Enter their details, assign their Role (Teacher, Driver, Librarian), and set their base salary.

### 3.2. Managing Leave Requests
1. Navigate to **HR** -> **Leave Management**.
2. Review pending leave applications from teachers and staff.
3. Approve or Reject the requests. Approved leaves will automatically factor into the end-of-month payroll.

### 3.3. Processing Payroll
1. Navigate to **HR** -> **Payroll**.
2. Select the current month.
3. The system automatically calculates salaries based on base pay, attendance (absences), and approved leaves.
4. Click **Generate Payslips** to create printable/downloadable salary slips for all staff.

---

## 4. The Finance / Accountant Workflow

The Finance team manages incoming revenue and tracks student fees.

### 4.1. Collecting Student Fees
1. Navigate to the **Finance** module.
2. Search for the student by Name or ID.
3. Select the fee type (e.g., Tuition, Transport).
4. Enter the amount received and the payment method (Cash, Bank Transfer).
5. Click **Record Payment**. The system will automatically generate a digital receipt that can be printed or emailed to the parents.

### 4.2. Tracking Outstanding Dues
1. Navigate to **Finance** -> **Outstanding Dues**.
2. Use the filters to find students who have not paid their fees for the current term.
3. Use the bulk notification feature (if enabled) to send WhatsApp or SMS reminders directly to their parents.

---

## 5. Operations: Library & Transport

### 5.1. Issuing Library Books
1. Navigate to the **Library** module.
2. Search for the book using its ISBN or Title.
3. Search for the Student ID.
4. Click **Issue Book**. The system tracks the return date and automatically calculates late fines if returned past the due date.

### 5.2. Assigning Transport Routes
1. Navigate to the **Transport** module.
2. Add the student to a specific bus route.
3. This action automatically updates the Finance module to include the corresponding Transport Fee on the student's next invoice.
